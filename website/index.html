03 GET STARTED

A few minutes of setup.
Then, your first cut.

Cutroom runs your installed coding agent. You’ll need a VideoDB account and a signed-in agent CLI on this computer.

  1. 1

    Install your agent.
    Sign in through its CLI.

    A ChatGPT or Claude website login alone won’t connect it to Cutroom.

    claude auth login
    codex login
    Install Claude Code ↗Install Codex ↗

    Choose either agent. Once CLI login succeeds, you can close the terminal.

  2. 2

    Connect VideoDB.
    Choose your account.

    Open Cutroom and select Sign in with VideoDB. Finish the browser sign-in and return to the app. No API key to copy.

    Cutroom↗VideoDB

    VideoDB powers media processing and rendering. Usage is billed to your connected VideoDB account.

  3. 3

    Bring your footage.
    Describe the edit.

    Create a project, import a video and choose your agent. Give it a brief, review the preview and refine the timeline before exporting.

    “Find the strongest moment in this video and help me turn it into a short.”

    Your project files stay on your computer. Imported media is processed in VideoDB.

04 A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you
hit record.

How is this different from a VideoDB plugin?

A plugin gives an agent video tools. Cutroom adds the visual workspace: media library, player, editable timeline, styling controls and project history alongside the conversation.

Do I need a separate agent subscription?

Cutroom uses your own installed Claude Code, Codex or Pi CLI and its existing authentication. Your agent’s account requirements and usage limits still apply. VideoDB processing uses your connected VideoDB account.

Can I use other tools with my agent?

Your coding agent can use its other configured tools. VideoDB work inside Cutroom follows the account connected in the app, so another VideoDB skill’s credentials should not replace it.

Are LUTs and templates included?

Nine subtitle presets and eight entrance/exit motion presets are included. Colour grading supports importing your own 3D .cube LUT files; a stock LUT library is not bundled.

Does everything run locally?

The desktop workspace and project files are local. Your agent uses its provider, while VideoDB handles uploaded media, transcription, search and rendering in the cloud.
